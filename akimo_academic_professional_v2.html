Research focus

Core areas of analytical work.

Akimo focuses on questions where energy-system decisions require more than one type of evidence. The objective is to integrate technical, spatial, economic, environmental, and policy dimensions into analysis that is easier to interpret and use.

01

Renewable energy analysis

Assessment of renewable-resource potential, spatial constraints, infrastructure, and implementation conditions relevant to clean-energy deployment.

02

Green hydrogen intelligence

Evaluation of hydrogen production pathways, costs, location suitability, electricity conditions, deployment scenarios, and enabling policy environments.

03

Decarbonisation pathways

Scenario-based analysis of transition options and the technical, economic, and institutional conditions that influence implementation.

04

Climate and energy policy

Comparative and evidence-based analysis of market structures, policy signals, investment conditions, and barriers affecting clean-energy adoption.

Analytical approach

From fragmented data to structured evidence.

Clean-energy decisions often depend on multiple interacting variables. Akimo combines complementary analytical methods to improve interpretation, reduce false precision, and make assumptions and trade-offs more explicit.

Evidence to action

Technical depth with practical interpretation.

The goal is not only to identify theoretical potential, but to examine the conditions that determine whether clean-energy options can become viable, investable, and implementable.

01

Geospatial modelling

Resource, location, infrastructure, and environmental suitability analysis.

02

Techno-economic assessment

Cost structures, technology assumptions, and deployment conditions.

03

Scenario and uncertainty analysis

Alternative pathways, sensitivity to assumptions, and decision-relevant ranges.

04

Policy and market interpretation

Connecting quantitative findings with institutional and implementation realities.

Selected research themes

Regional questions, country-specific evidence.

Akimo's work is particularly interested in clean-energy transitions in Latin America, where strong renewable resources coexist with financing, infrastructure, market, and policy constraints that can slow implementation.

Costa Rica

Renewable systems and green hydrogen

Analysis of clean-energy transition opportunities, hydrogen deployment conditions, renewable-resource advantages, and the relationship between technical potential and implementation.

Mexico

Spatial and techno-economic hydrogen analysis

State-level and location-based assessment of green hydrogen potential, including spatial suitability, cost conditions, and scenario-based comparison.

Comparative analysis

Markets, policy, and transition conditions

Comparative examination of electricity-market structures, tariffs, policy frameworks, risk conditions, and other factors shaping clean-energy deployment.

Why Akimo

Bridging the evidence-to-action gap.

Important energy decisions are frequently made across fragmented datasets, technical reports, policy documents, cost assumptions, and spatial constraints. Akimo's role is to organize that complexity into a more coherent analytical picture.

01 · Integrate Bring technical, economic, spatial, environmental, and policy evidence into one analytical frame.
02 · Interpret Translate complex findings into clearer implications, trade-offs, and decision-relevant insights.
03 · Inform Support more evidence-based discussion among researchers, policymakers, investors, and other stakeholders.
About the initiative

Research-led. Independent. Regionally grounded.

Akimo Energy Analytics is designed as a research and analytical platform for clean-energy questions that sit between academic research and real-world implementation.

Mission

To produce rigorous, accessible, and context-specific analysis that improves understanding of clean-energy opportunities, constraints, and transition pathways.

Regional orientation

A particular emphasis is placed on Costa Rica and Latin America, while drawing on international evidence where comparative analysis can improve local interpretation.

Long-term direction

Akimo aims to strengthen the connection between quantitative research, public-facing communication, partnerships, and decision-support resources for energy and climate action.

Collaboration

Research, analysis, and clean-energy dialogue.

Akimo welcomes research collaboration and exchange related to renewable energy, green hydrogen, decarbonisation, geospatial analysis, and energy-transition policy.
